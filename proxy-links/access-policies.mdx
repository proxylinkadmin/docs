---
title: Access Policies
description: Control who can access each proxy link.
---

Every proxy link has an access policy that controls who can reach it.

## Policy types

### ProxyLink sign-in
The link opens only for someone signed in to ProxyLink who is entitled to that device, from
their dashboard. This is the default for new web links, and the right choice for almost all of
them.

### Public
Anyone with the URL can access the link. Suitable for services you want to share openly.

### VPN-only
Only a client connected to the ProxyLink VPN can access the link, and only one belonging to the
same account or team as the link. The proxy rejects all other requests.

### IP allowlist
Only requests from the exact IP addresses you list are allowed (one IP per entry - CIDR ranges are not currently supported).

```
198.51.100.24
203.0.113.42
```

### Password protected
A password prompt appears before the proxied service is accessible.

## Default policy

New web links (HTTP/HTTPS) default to **ProxyLink sign-in** - they are not reachable by anyone
who is not signed in and entitled to the device. Change the policy from the link's edit page or
directly from the proxy link list.

TCP and UDP links are different: they never pass through a sign-in page, so they offer
**VPN-only**, **IP allowlist** and **Public** only, and default to VPN-only.

## What VPN-only actually checks

VPN-only checks the **source IP**: the request must arrive from inside the ProxyLink WireGuard
range, from a device or member peer belonging to the same account or team as the link. It does
not consult a browser session, so being signed in to ProxyLink makes no difference to it either
way - and a peer of a different tenant is refused even though it is on the VPN.

If what you want is "only someone signed in to ProxyLink", that is the **ProxyLink sign-in**
policy above, not this one.
